# v1.6 Tactical Adjustment Backtest

**Run date:** 2026-06-21  
**Fixture window:** 2026-06-11 through 2026-06-21  
**Available matches:** 39  
**Evaluated matches:** 35  
**Method:** For each completed match, rebuild recent form, current Elo, and tactical profiles using only events strictly before kickoff. Compare the same model with and without tactical adjustments.

| Market | Baseline accuracy | Tactical accuracy | Baseline Brier | Tactical Brier | Baseline log loss | Tactical log loss |
|--------|------------------:|------------------:|---------------:|---------------:|------------------:|------------------:|
| 1X2 | 60.0% (21/35) | 57.1% (20/35) | 0.5591 | 0.5589 | 0.9338 | 0.9327 |

| BTTS | 60.0% (21/35) | 60.0% (21/35) | 0.2289 | 0.2309 | 0.6489 | 0.6530 |

## Conclusion

Tactical adjustments did **not** increase observed accuracy in this initial sample. They slightly improved probability calibration for 1X2 and Over 2.5, but reduced classification accuracy by one match in each market. BTTS accuracy was unchanged and its probability scores worsened. The tactical layer should not be described as an accuracy improvement without a larger chronological sample or further weight calibration.

## Coverage Gaps

Four matches were excluded because fewer than three stat-complete tactical summaries were available before kickoff. No neutral or synthetic tactical profiles were substituted.
