# Plan 20-01: Leakage-Safe Historical Tactical Dataset

## Objective

Create a reproducible training table of historical senior international matches whose baseline and tactical features are reconstructed strictly from information available before kickoff.

## Tasks

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Prove source coverage before modeling</name>
  <files>alpha/data/ingestion/wc_tactical_history.py, tests/unit/data/test_wc_tactical_history.py</files>
  <action>Implement a read-only coverage audit that enumerates candidate senior international competitions, ESPN schedule/summary endpoints, unique event counts, required tactical-field coverage, card-status coverage, profile depth, and projected development/validation/audit counts. Persist a coverage manifest. Block downstream dataset construction unless at least 200 development, 50 later pre-tournament validation, and 30 frozen 2026 World Cup audit rows are attainable; never lower these gates automatically.</action>
  <verify>Run `pytest tests/unit/data/test_wc_tactical_history.py -q`; fixtures cover sufficient, insufficient, duplicate, missing-card, and malformed endpoint coverage.</verify>
  <acceptance>Modeling cannot start on assumed coverage, and a failure reports exactly which source or field prevents the declared sample gates.</acceptance>
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: Define contracts and build strict as-of rows</name>
  <files>alpha/data/ingestion/wc_tactical_history.py, scripts/build_wc_tactical_dataset.py, tests/unit/data/test_wc_tactical_history.py</files>
  <action>Introduce versioned row, exclusion, and metadata contracts, then enumerate and deduplicate audited events. Include event ID, kickoff, competition, teams, score, as-of Elo and its source date, as-of goal-rate priors and source window, baseline 1X2/lambdas, seven tactical components, profile depth, source timestamps, card status, neutral/extra-time flags, and exclusion reason. Rebuild profiles only from events completed before kickoff. Exclude known red cards, extra time, missing card status, insufficient samples, missing scores, future-data contamination, and unsupported competitions. Write immutable raw snapshots separately from deterministic derived rows.</action>
  <verify>Run the focused tests plus a small fixture build twice and assert byte-identical rows/manifest; add a test where a future match with extreme statistics cannot affect an earlier row.</verify>
  <acceptance>No row can observe its own or a later match, all exclusions carry reasons, and reruns over unchanged cache data are deterministic.</acceptance>
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 3: Seal validation and external-audit manifests</name>
  <files>scripts/build_wc_tactical_dataset.py, scripts/validate_wc_tactics.py, tests/unit/engines/test_validate_wc_tactics.py</files>
  <action>Add CLI summaries for discovered, eligible, excluded, development, validation, and external-audit counts. Freeze at least 50 latest eligible pre-2026-06-11 matches as chronological validation. Freeze eligible World Cup events from 2026-06-11 through the manifest creation cutoff, with explicit event IDs and at least 30 rows, as external audit.   <verify>Run `pytest tests/unit/data/test_wc_tactical_history.py tests/unit/engines/test_validate_wc_tactics.py -q` and a cached-data dry run of `python scripts/build_wc_tactical_dataset.py --report-only`.</verify>
  <acceptance>The report makes coverage limitations visible and the holdout cannot enter a development export.</acceptance>
</task>

A versioned historical dataset and manifest suitable for chronological training without contaminating the World Cup holdout.
