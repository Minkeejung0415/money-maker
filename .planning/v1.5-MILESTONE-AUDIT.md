# v1.5 Milestone Audit

## Result

PASS. All 12 requirements are implemented and covered by focused tests. Both phases have summaries and verification evidence, and the full 753-test suite passes.

## Integration

- WC Elo probabilities feed the scoreline calibration contract.
- Historical WC team stats feed bounded attack and defense goal rates.
- Normalized override prices feed the true-SGP builder without defaults.
- Scanner mode selects true same-game or classic cross-game construction explicitly.
- Knockout settlement semantics prevent advance probabilities from being mixed with regulation-time 1X2 legs.

## Residual Risk

The repository has no live free source for World Cup totals or BTTS prices. Those prices must be entered in `data/wc_odds_override.json`. The model remains usable for probability calculation, but it correctly emits no betting edge when compatible prices are absent. Products of standalone odds are indicative; actual sportsbook SGP quotes can differ because books price correlation.
